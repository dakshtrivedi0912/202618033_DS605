## Key Observations:-

1. Female passengers had a higher survival rate than male passengers.

2. First-class passengers had the highest survival rate.

3. Third-class passengers had the lowest survival rate.

4. Higher fares were associated with higher survival rates.

5. Fare contained several outliers.

6. Age had a weaker relationship with survival than sex and passenger class.
